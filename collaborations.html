Collaborations

Joint work recorded in the 2025–26 activity report, plus ongoing state engagement in Kerala.

National Institute of Virology (NIV), Pune

Cost-effectiveness of MMR vaccination in India

Collaborative HTA. Protocol presented to TAC; preliminary report 6 Nov 2025; completed study at the 60th TAC (3 Dec 2025). Revision underway following NTAGI comments.

Ongoing

PGIMER Chandigarh (HTA Resource Hub)

Costing of Health Systems in India — II (CHSI 2.0)

SCTIMST is a collaborating centre for a policy-responsive national cost database. Kerala sites: Government Medical College, Trivandrum (neurology, endocrinology, gastroenterology, nephrology) and district hospitals in Trivandrum and Kollam. Team trained at PGIMER (10–14 Feb 2026). Ethics and DME/DHS permissions obtained; tri-partite MoA in signing; data collection started.
