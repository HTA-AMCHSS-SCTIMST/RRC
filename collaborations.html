Collaborations

RRC-HTA works with partner institutions on joint HTA and health-systems modelling. Further partners can be added as agreements are documented.

All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), Bhopal

Joint health-systems and spatial-access modelling between RRC-HTA and AIIMS Bhopal.

One Block One Dialysis (OBOD)

Spatial accessibility and equity analysis for dialysis facility planning, using two-step floating catchment area (2SFCA) methods alongside health-systems modelling to inform facility allocation and coverage decisions.
