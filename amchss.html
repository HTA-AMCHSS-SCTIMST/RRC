Achutha Menon Centre for Health Science Studies

AMCHSS is the public health wing of SCTIMST. The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India, recognises it as a centre of excellence for public health training.

With AMCHSS, SCTIMST uniquely combines tertiary care, biomedical technology research, and health science training. The centre’s main objective is to train health professionals in health research and in the formulation and implementation of health policy.

Academic programmes

  • Master of Public Health (MPH) — international programme serving South Asia
  • Diploma in Public Health (DPH)
  • PhD (regular and part-time)
  • Short courses in public health methods, informatics, GIS and HTA

Research focus

Faculty work on non-communicable diseases, gender and health, health policy and management, infectious disease epidemiology, health data science, and health technology assessment. AMCHSS has collaborations with universities including Arizona, Melbourne, Monash, Edinburgh and Heidelberg, and is an ICMR Advanced Centre for Clinical Trials.

The HTAIn Regional Resource Centre sits within this academic environment so that HTA studies can be supervised to INI standards and can feed into teaching.
