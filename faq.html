Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is this centre?

It is the HTAIn Regional Resource Centre at AMCHSS, SCTIMST, Trivandrum. HTAIn is the national Health Technology Assessment office under DHR. This centre conducts assessments and coordinates with the Government of Kerala.

2. What is the purpose of HTA?

To inform evidence-based decisions on whether a health technology is clinically worthwhile, safe, cost-effective and affordable, so resources can be allocated fairly.

3. What technologies are assessed?

Medicines, devices, diagnostics, surgical procedures, public health programmes, clinical interventions and digital health (including AI), as allocated or submitted through HTAIn.

4. Who uses HTA outputs?

Central and state health departments, insurance and public programmes (including pathways linked to Ayushman Bharat at national level), and other policy users identified by HTAIn.

5. How does this differ from CDSCO?

CDSCO licenses and regulates products for safety and quality. HTAIn does not license products; it assesses value for health-system decisions after or alongside regulation.

6. How do I submit a topic?

Use the national Topic Submission Portal on htain.dhr.gov.in. You may also write to this centre for scientific scoping help after submission or allocation.

7. Can AMCHSS decide what Kerala will purchase?

No. The resource centre produces evidence. National TAC/Board processes and state government decisions remain with those authorities.

8. How is HTA linked to Universal Health Coverage?

By helping programmes choose interventions that improve health within budget, HTA supports UHC goals of access, quality and financial protection.
