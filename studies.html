Health Technology Assessment of Pneumococcal Conjugate Vaccine (PCV) to Prevent Mortality and Morbidity of Pneumococcal Disease in Indian Adults
Cost-utility analysis using Markov models comparing PCV vaccination scenarios (single-dose PCV13 and serial PCV13/PPSV23) against standard care across four adult age groups. Vaccination found cost-effective in adults aged 65+, most favourable in the 75+ cohort. Authors: Biju Soman, Antony Stanley, Ashis John, Adrija Roy, Abhilash Kurup — RRC-HTA, AMCHSS, SCTIMST.		 2023 Vaccines Completed HTAIn PDF
