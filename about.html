About RRC-HTA

This page describes RRC-HTA’s role in the HTAIn. For national mandate, governance and objectives, see htain.dhr.gov.in.

The Regional Resource Centre for Health Technology Assessment (RRC-HTAIn) was established at AMCHSS, SCTIMST, Trivandrum on 27 February 2018. It is funded by the Department of Health Research. The mandate is to conduct research, build capacity, and generate quality evidence to support health-policy decisions in Kerala towards Universal Health Coverage.

Mandate

HTAIn operates under the Department of Health Research, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, and generates evidence on the clinical effectiveness, cost-effectiveness and safety of medicines, devices and health programmes.

SCTIMST coordinates with the State Government of Kerala. Prof. Biju Soman is Principal Investigator. In 2025–26 the centre received the gold medal for best-performing resource centre at ARISE-2025 (10 December 2025, New Delhi), accepted by Prof. Biju Soman from Prof. V.K. Paul, NITI Aayog.

Relationship to AMCHSS, SCTIMST and HTAIn

  • AMCHSS is recognised as a centre of excellence for public health training by MoHFW and hosts RRC-HTA’s research team.
  • SCTIMST is the parent Institute of National Importance (DST).
  • HTAIn allocates and oversees multi-centric HTA studies through Resource Hubs and Resource Centres. Board and TAC details are on the national site.

What the unit does

  • Cost-utility analysis (CUA) — cost per QALY gained.
  • Budget impact analysis (BIA) — financial impact of adopting technologies.
  • Decision-analytic modelling — Markov models, decision trees and infectious-disease models in R (heemod, hesim), TreeAge and Excel.
  • Capacity-building — sensitisation sessions and workshops. See Events.

Priorities ahead include completing ongoing HTAs to HTAIn quality standards, expanding free and open-source software for literature review and modelling, and supporting a state HTA cell in Kerala. See Resources.
