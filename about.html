About the HTA Resource Centre

The Health Technology Assessment Regional Resource Centre at SCTIMST is hosted by the Achutha Menon Centre for Health Science Studies (AMCHSS). It is part of the national HTAIn network created by the Department of Health Research, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India.

Mandate

Resource Centres conduct HTA and multi-centric studies allocated by the HTAIn Secretariat. As recorded in HTAIn guidelines, SCTIMST, Trivandrum coordinates with the State Government of Kerala.

The Regional Technical Resource Centre for HTAIn at SCTIMST was supported by DHR (Principal Investigator: Prof. Biju Soman). The centre works at the intersection of public health, health economics, epidemiology and health policy — strengths of AMCHSS — and can draw on SCTIMST’s hospital and biomedical technology wings when topics involve devices or specialty care.

Objectives

  • Generate HTA evidence on clinical effectiveness, safety, cost-effectiveness and budget impact.
  • Support Kerala’s health system and national programmes with independent appraisals.
  • Follow HTAIn process: protocol, systematic review, economic evaluation, stakeholder consultation, and reporting to the Technical Appraisal Committee and HTAIn Board as required.
  • Build a pool of HTA researchers in the region through MPH/PhD teaching and short courses.

Institutional home

SCTIMST is an Institution of National Importance under the Department of Science and Technology (Act No. 52 of 1980). AMCHSS is recognised by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare as a centre of excellence for public health training. Hosting HTAIn here places technology assessment in a university-status institute that already combines patient care, device development and public health.
