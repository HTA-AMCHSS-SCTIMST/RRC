About RRC-HTA

This page describes RRC-HTA’s role in the HTAIn network. For national mandate, governance and objectives, see htain.dhr.gov.in.

The Regional Resource Centre for Health Technology Assessment (RRC-HTA) is one of the Resource Centres under HTAIn — institutions established in research institutes to conduct HTA and other multi-centric studies allocated by the HTAIn Secretariat. It is hosted at AMCHSS, SCTIMST, Thiruvananthapuram.

Mandate

HTAIn operates under the Department of Health Research, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, and generates evidence on the clinical effectiveness, cost-effectiveness and safety of medicines, devices and health programmes for transparent, evidence-informed policy.

As recorded in HTAIn guidelines, SCTIMST, Trivandrum coordinates with the State Government of Kerala. The Regional Technical Resource Centre for HTAIn at SCTIMST was supported by DHR (Principal Investigator: Prof. Biju Soman).

Relationship to AMCHSS, SCTIMST and HTAIn

  • AMCHSS is recognised as a centre of excellence for public health training by MoHFW and hosts RRC-HTA’s research team.
  • SCTIMST is the parent Institute of National Importance (DST).
  • HTAIn allocates and oversees multi-centric HTA studies through Resource Hubs and Resource Centres. Board and TAC details are on the national site.

What the unit does

  • Cost-utility analysis (CUA) — cost per QALY gained.
  • Budget impact analysis (BIA) — financial impact of adopting technologies.
  • Decision-analytic modelling — Markov models, decision trees and infectious-disease models in R (heemod, hesim), TreeAge and Excel.
  • Capacity-building — sensitisation sessions and workshops. See Events.

Studies are commissioned or allocated through the HTAIn Secretariat and follow HTAIn methodological guidelines, process manual and costing manual. See Resources.
