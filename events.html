RRC-HTA organises consultative meetings, methodology workshops, and capacity-building programmes to support health technology assessment and evidence-informed policymaking in Kerala and across India.

State Consultative Meetings

State Consultative Meeting on HTA, Kerala ↗

31 January 2026 · Thiruvananthapuram
An in-person consultative workshop organised for state health officials, supported by HTAIn, New Delhi, and the Department of Health and Family Welfare, Government of Kerala. Attended by 42 senior officials from DHS, DME, KMSCL, and SHSRC to strengthen collaboration with state authorities.

State Consultative Meeting for HTA ↗

25 September 2025 · Thiruvananthapuram
Organised for senior officials of the Kerala Health System to introduce key HTA concepts and highlight its value in state-level decision-making. Felicitated by Dr. Vinay Goel IAS (SMD, NHM-Kerala), Dr. Bipin Gopal (Deputy Director, DHS), and Dr. Premalatha T.K. (Joint Director, DME). Attended by officials from DHS, DME, NHM, SHSRC, and neighbouring HTA centres.

Value and Use of HTA In Health Policy-Making for Kerala

27–28 November 2019 · SCTIMST
Interactive workshop conducted for over 20 senior healthcare professionals and programme managers from Kerala to explore how HTA can be systematically integrated into the state's health policy-making process.

Workshops & Training Organised by RRC-HTA

Workshop on R for HTA (SCTIMST) ↗

9–10 September 2025 · SCTIMST
A two-day intensive training workshop on R programming tailored for Health Technology Assessment, training ~30 technical staff from HTA resource centres across India in decision trees, Markov modelling, probabilistic sensitivity analysis (PSA), and cost-effectiveness visualisations.

HTA for Effective Decision-Making: Equipping Kerala's Programme Managers

October 2021 – March 2022 · Hybrid / SCTIMST
Designed for 14 Medical Officers across Kerala districts serving as mid-level programme managers. Utilised a hybrid structure with bi-monthly online sessions (Oct–Feb) followed by a culminating 3-day hands-on physical workshop at AMCHSS, SCTIMST.

Basics of HTA Module in the MPH Curriculum

Since 2021 · AMCHSS, SCTIMST
A specialized module integrated into the Master of Public Health (MPH) curriculum at SCTIMST, introducing public health students to the fundamentals and principles of Health Technology Assessment.

Workshop on Critical Appraisal of Literature, Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

25–27 July 2018 · SCTIMST
Three-day methodology workshop convened with 24 healthcare professionals from Kerala and other Indian states on literature appraisal and systematic evidence synthesis.

Data Exploration using R for Healthcare Professionals

16–19 May 2018 · SCTIMST
A four-day training programme on statistical data exploration using R for 20 healthcare professionals from government (DHS and DME) and private healthcare sectors.
