Events

Capacity-building listed in the Report of Activities 2025–26. Workshop photographs can be added to assets/gallery/ when consent is cleared.

Organised by RRC-HTA

Workshop on R for Health Technology Assessment

9–10 September 2025, SCTIMST. Two-day training for about 30 technical staff from HTA resource centres across India: decision trees, Markov modelling, probabilistic sensitivity analysis, and cost-effectiveness visualisation in R.

State consultative meeting on HTA

25 September 2025. Workshop for senior Kerala health-system officials (DHS, DME, NHM, SHSRC and neighbouring-state HTA centres). Felicitated by Dr. Vinay Goel IAS (SMD, Kerala), Dr. Bipin Gopal (DHS) and Dr. Premalatha T.K. (DME). Led to the idea of a state HTA cell.

State consultative meeting on HTA (follow-up)

31 January 2026, Thiruvananthapuram. Supported by HTAIn and the Department of Health and Family Welfare, Government of Kerala. Forty-two senior officials from DHS, DME, KMSCL and SHSRC.

Attended by RRC members

ADOPT 2025 — national sensitisation on HTA

20 August 2025, Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi. Attended by Prof. Biju Soman, Dr. Ashis Samuel John and Dr. Rehna C. Mohamed, with Kerala NHM representatives.

Bioeconomy and HTA 2026

19 January 2026, IIT Guwahati (HTAIn with IIPH Shillong). Prof. Biju Soman spoke on FOSS tools for HTAIn. Posters by Dr. Ashis Samuel John and Dr. Rehna C. Mohamed.

4th Regional Workshop on Advanced Health Economic Evaluation

23–25 February 2026, JIPMER Puducherry. Dr. Ashis Samuel John and Dr. Rehna C. Mohamed.

R for HTA (Basics)

17–19 March 2026, AIIMS Bhopal. Dr. Akhil Sasidharan (session on probabilistic sensitivity analysis) and Dr. Rehna C. Mohamed.
