Welcome to the HTA Resource Centre at AMCHSS

To support transparent and evidence-informed decision making in health, the Government of India established Health Technology Assessment in India (HTAIn) under the Department of Health Research. SCTIMST, Trivandrum is one of the designated Regional Resource Centres of HTAIn. The centre is hosted at the Achutha Menon Centre for Health Science Studies (AMCHSS) and coordinates HTA work with the State Government of Kerala.

We assess medicines, devices, diagnostics, surgical and public-health interventions, and digital health tools for safety, clinical effectiveness, cost-effectiveness and affordability — so that quality care can be delivered within available resources.

What this centre does

Assess technologies

Systematic review of clinical and economic evidence for interventions relevant to Kerala and to topics allocated by the HTAIn Secretariat.

Inform state policy

Coordinate with the Department of Health and Family Welfare, Government of Kerala, and state programmes that need HTA inputs.

Build capacity

Train researchers, programme managers and students in HTA methods, health economics and evidence-informed policy.
