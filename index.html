About RRC-HTA

The Regional Resource Centre for Health Technology Assessment (RRC-HTA) is based at the Achutha Menon Centre for Health Science Studies (AMCHSS), Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute for Medical Sciences and Technology (SCTIMST), Thiruvananthapuram. It is one of the Resource Centres under HTAIn, Department of Health Research, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India.

The unit conducts health economics and HTA work — cost-utility analysis, budget impact analysis, and decision-analytic modelling for the Indian health system — and runs sensitisation sessions and workshops as part of capacity-building.

What this centre does

Cost-utility analysis

Evaluate interventions as cost per quality-adjusted life year (QALY) gained in the Indian context.

Budget impact

Project the financial effect of adopting technologies within Indian health-system budgets.

Decision-analytic modelling

Markov models, decision trees and infectious-disease models in R (heemod, hesim), TreeAge and Excel.
