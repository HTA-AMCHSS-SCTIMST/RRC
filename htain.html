Health Technology Assessment in India (HTAIn)

HTAIn is an attached office under the Department of Health Research, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. It maintains a network of resource hubs and resource centres to evaluate technologies mainly for clinical effectiveness and cost-effectiveness.

Clinical effectiveness reviews safety and clinical performance. Cost-effectiveness reviews value for money and affordability for the population. HTAIn receives requests to review medicines, medical and diagnostic devices, surgical interventions, population-level public health programmes, clinical interventions and digital health (including AI).

Governance (national)

  • HTAIn Board — apex decision-making body
  • Technical Appraisal Committee (TAC) — scientific appraisal
  • Secretariat (DHR) — coordination with user departments, TAC, technical partners and resource centres
  • Resource Hubs and Resource Centres — including SCTIMST, Trivandrum

This website is the local face of the SCTIMST Resource Centre. National policy, topic allocation, TAC/Board decisions and the official topic submission portal remain on htain.dhr.gov.in.
