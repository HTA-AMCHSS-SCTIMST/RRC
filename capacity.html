Capacity Building

AMCHSS runs degree programmes and short courses that strengthen HTA skills in the region — systematic review, health economics, epidemiology, and health data science.

Degree teaching

HTA, public health technologies, infectious disease epidemiology and database management are taught in MPH and PhD programmes.

Short courses

Health technology assessment, use of R for health data, GIS in public health, bibliographic tools and public health informatics.
