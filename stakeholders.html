For Government Stakeholders & Innovators

Government programmes

Health Technology Assessment gives state and national programmes independent evidence on value and impact of technologies. Kerala departments and programme officers who wish a technology to be reviewed should register on the national HTAIn Topic Submission Portal (short questionnaire). The same login can be reused. Review status is visible on the portal.

Innovators and industry

HTA helps validate technologies, support adoption in a cost-sensitive system, and align products with patient and payer needs. Innovators should submit through the HTAIn portal and be ready with:

EvidenceWhat is needed
RegulatoryCDSCO or other applicable approvals; quality standards
Product detailsSpecifications, infrastructure, training, lifecycle cost
Clinical evidenceTrials, real-world data, comparative studies; systematic review or manuscript if unpublished
Stakeholder feedbackProvider/user input, pilots if available

This resource centre does not replace CDSCO regulation. HTAIn is not a licensing authority; it informs coverage and policy decisions.
