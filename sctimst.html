Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute for Medical Sciences & Technology

SCTIMST, Trivandrum, is an Institution of National Importance under the Department of Science and Technology, Government of India (Act No. 52 of 1980). It has university status and a unique mandate that combines biomedical technologies, advanced specialty care, higher education, research and public health.

Three wings

Hospital Wing

Tertiary super-specialty care, especially cardiac and neurosciences, with postgraduate training.

Biomedical Technology Wing

Development of medical devices and biomaterials — directly relevant when HTA topics involve indigenous technologies.

AMCHSS

Public health school hosting the HTAIn Regional Resource Centre.
