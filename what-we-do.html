What We Do

Following the national HTAIn mandate, this resource centre reviews and evaluates health technologies for clinical effectiveness (safety and clinical performance) and cost-effectiveness (value for money and affordability).

Topics may be allocated by the HTAIn Secretariat or arise from Kerala government programmes. Types of technologies include medicines, medical and diagnostic devices, surgical interventions, population-level public health interventions, clinical interventions, and digital health including artificial intelligence.

Evidence synthesis

Systematic reviews, critical appraisal and contextualisation of clinical evidence for India and Kerala.

Economic evaluation

Cost-effectiveness, cost-utility and budget-impact analyses using HTAIn methods guidance.

Stakeholder engagement

Consultation with programmes, clinicians, patients and state departments before recommendations are finalised nationally.

HTA of newer technologies supports affordable, quality healthcare within available resources and optimal allocation — including pathways such as Ayushman Bharat where national HTAIn outputs are used.
