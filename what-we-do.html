What We Do

RRC-HTA reviews health technologies for clinical effectiveness and cost-effectiveness, using HTAIn methods. Topics are allocated by the HTAIn Secretariat or arise from Kerala programmes.

Cost-utility analysis

Markov and related models reporting incremental cost per QALY in the Indian public-system context.

Budget impact analysis

Affordability of vaccines, devices and programmes for national and state budgets.

Decision-analytic modelling

TreeAge and Excel for cohort, multi-state and sensitivity analyses (including PSA and ICERs).

Technologies assessed include medicines, devices, diagnostics, surgical and public-health interventions, and digital health. Outputs support resource allocation; they do not replace CDSCO licensing or HTAIn Board decisions.
