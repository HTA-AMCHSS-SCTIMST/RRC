HTA Process

The centre follows the national HTAIn process. Resource centres do not take final coverage decisions; they produce assessment reports that move through TAC and the HTAIn Board.

1Topic

Submitted on the HTAIn portal or allocated by the Secretariat.

2Protocol

Research question, PICO, methods and timelines agreed.

3Assessment

Clinical review, costing, modelling and equity considerations.

4Consultation

Stakeholder comments and revision of the draft report.

5Appraisal

TAC review and Board endorsement at national level.

User departments (central or state) and innovators should use the HTAIn Topic Submission Portal. Status of a technology under review is tracked on that portal. This Kerala resource centre can advise on scoping and data needs once a topic is assigned.
